Spiti is what happens when you keep driving past the last familiar thing. The
land turns bare, the air gets thin, and every village feels like it was built to
survive winter rather than welcome visitors.

The roads are the point. Long stretches of nothing, a river carving beside you,
and monasteries that have watched it all for a thousand years.
