Rajasthan is loud in the best way — colour everywhere, forts stacked on hills,
and bazaars that never seem to close. You come for the architecture and stay for
the evenings, when the desert cools and the sandstone glows.

Jodhpur's blue lanes and Udaipur's lakes are the two I keep thinking about.
